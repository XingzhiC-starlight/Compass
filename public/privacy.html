Compass Privacy Notice

Compass is a private workspace for easy project management. The beta version stores only the data needed to run your account, sync your projects, and protect the app.

What Compass Stores

  • Your account email address and the sign-in method you used.
  • Google OAuth identity information if you choose Continue with Google.
  • Your project setup interview answers and project names.
  • Your dashboard content: phases, learning items, tasks, time sessions, and thoughts.
  • Auth and security metadata needed for login, abuse prevention, and troubleshooting.

How Compass Uses Your Data

Compass uses your data to show your dashboard, keep it synced across devices, authenticate your account, send account emails, and keep the service reliable. We do not sell your data or use your project or thought content for advertising.

Compass does not currently use AI features, third-party analytics, or error logging that captures the text of your thoughts, tasks, or project reflections. If that changes, this notice should be reviewed before the feature reaches users.

Who Supports The App

Compass is supported by a small set of infrastructure providers:

  • Supabase for authentication, database storage, and row-level security.
  • Vercel for hosting the web app.
  • Resend for confirmation and password-reset emails.
  • Cloudflare Turnstile for bot and abuse protection on email auth.
  • Google OAuth when you choose Google sign-in.

Privacy And Access

Your Compass rows are tied to your signed-in user account. Database row-level security is enabled so one user cannot read another user's projects, thoughts, tasks, or time history. The production site and Supabase connection use HTTPS.

Beta Limits

During this beta, Compass does not offer data export, account deletion, or a public support mailbox. Those controls should be added deliberately before a wider release.
